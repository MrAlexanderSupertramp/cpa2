Frequently Asked Questions

  • What accounting software do you use?
    We are proficient in various top accounting software including QuickBooks (Online and Desktop), and Xero to provide flexible and efficient services.
  • How do I share my financial documents with you?
    We utilize secure cloud-based platforms and portals for easy and safe document sharing. We'll guide you through the simple onboarding process.
  • What's the difference between accounting and bookkeeping?
    Bookkeeping involves the recording of daily financial transactions. Accounting is a higher-level process that uses bookkeeping data to analyze, interpret, and report on your business's financial health, often including strategic advice.
  • How often will I receive financial reports?
    We tailor our reporting frequency to your needs – typically monthly, quarterly, or annually. We ensure you receive timely information to make informed decisions.
  • Can you help with filing corporate taxes?
    Yes, comprehensive tax planning and preparation are key parts of our accounting services. We handle GST/HST/PST/Source deductions/WSIB filings and can assist with corporate income tax returns.
  • Do you offer services for newly incorporated businesses?
    Absolutely. We assist with new corporation setup, ensuring your accounting systems are established correctly from day one for compliance and efficiency.
  • How much do your accounting services cost?
    Our pricing is tailored to your specific needs and the complexity of your business. We offer transparent flat-rate or scalable hourly options after our initial consultation. Contact us for a custom quote.
  • How secure is my financial data with you?
    Data security is our top priority. We use industry-standard secure platforms, encryption, and follow strict confidentiality protocols to protect your sensitive financial information.
  • Who will be handling my account?
    You will be assigned a dedicated Account Manager and Accountant who will be your primary points of contact, ensuring consistent and personalized service.
