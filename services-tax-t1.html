Professional T1 personal income tax preparation for individuals, families, employees and self-employed Canadians.

At Herb Wodehouse Professional Corporation, we prepare T1 personal income tax returns for individuals and families. Whether your return is a single T4 slip or includes business, rental and investment income, we make sure every eligible deduction and credit is claimed and your return is filed correctly and on time.

We prepare returns using TaxPrep, professional tax software for Canadian personal, corporate and trust returns, and we file electronically with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).

Our Personal Tax Process

01 Document Collection

You send us your slips (T4, T5, T3, T5008, RRSP receipts), receipts and any CRA correspondence through our secure channels.

02 Preparation & Review

We prepare your return, identify deductions and credits, and review it carefully for accuracy before anything is filed.

03 Your Approval

We walk you through the return, explain any balance owing or refund, and obtain your signed authorization.

04 Filing & Follow-up

We e-file with the CRA and help with notices of assessment, reassessments and any follow-up requests.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • When is the personal tax filing deadline?
    Most individuals must file by April 30 for the previous year. If you or your spouse or common-law partner carry on a business, the filing deadline is June 15, but any balance owing is still due April 30.
  • What documents do I need to bring?
    Your tax slips (T4, T5, T3, T5008 and similar), RRSP and FHSA receipts, medical, tuition and donation receipts, business or rental records if applicable, and last year's notice of assessment.
  • Do you file self-employed and rental income?
    Yes. We prepare returns that include business, professional, commission and rental income, along with the related expenses.
  • Can you file returns for previous years?
    Yes. We can prepare and file late returns and catch up outstanding years, and help you deal with the CRA.
  • What if I receive a CRA notice or reassessment?
    Send it to us as soon as you receive it. We will review it, respond to the CRA on your behalf where authorized, and tell you what is required.
  • How do I send you my documents?
    We use secure channels for document sharing. We will guide you through the process when you contact us.
