Corporate Tax Returns
Filed Right and On Time

Dependable T2 corporate income tax preparation and filing for Canadian corporations of all sizes.

Every Canadian corporation must file a T2 corporate income tax return each year, even if there is no tax payable. Herb Wodehouse Professional Corporation prepares and files your T2 return with the required schedules, so it is accurate, on time and supported by your financial statements.

We prepare returns using TaxPrep and file electronically with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), covering both federal and Ontario corporate tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • When is my T2 return due?
    The T2 return is due six months after the end of your corporation's fiscal year. Any balance owing is generally due earlier, two months after year-end, or three months for eligible Canadian-controlled private corporations.
  • Do I have to file if my corporation had no activity?
    Yes. A T2 return is required for every corporation, even when there is no income or no tax payable.
  • What are the penalties for filing late?
    A late-filing penalty applies to any balance owing, and it increases if you were late before. Filing on time avoids it, so contact us early.
  • What do you need from me to prepare the T2?
    Your year-end financial statements or bookkeeping records, bank statements, details of shareholder loans, dividends and salary paid, and last year's return.
  • Can you help if my books are not up to date?
    Yes. We can bring your bookkeeping up to date and then prepare your return.
  • Can you help with a CRA review or audit?
    Yes. We can respond to CRA requests and correspondence on your behalf where you have authorized us.
