Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does outsourcing payroll save my business money?
    Outsourcing eliminates the need for dedicated payroll staff salaries, benefits, office space, software costs, and training. It also prevents costly errors and penalties associated with incorrect filings or calculations.
  • What information do I need to provide to get started?
    We'll need your business information (legal name, address, CRA business number), employee details (names, addresses, SINs, pay rates, deductions), and banking information for direct deposits and tax remittances.
  • How are payroll taxes handled?
    We accurately calculate all required federal and provincial payroll taxes (CPP, EI, income tax) and remit them to the appropriate government agencies (like the CRA) on your behalf by the deadlines.
  • Can you handle different pay schedules (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)?
    Yes, we can accommodate various pay frequencies, including weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, and monthly schedules, tailored to your company's needs.
  • How do employees receive their pay stubs?
    We provide secure online access for employees to view and download their pay stubs and year-end tax forms (T4s/T4As) at any time.
  • What about Records of Employment (ROEs)?
    We prepare and submit Records of Employment electronically to Service Canada whenever an employee experiences an interruption of earnings, ensuring compliance.
  • Is my payroll data secure?
    Absolutely. We use industry-leading secure software and encrypted communication channels, adhering to strict confidentiality protocols to protect your sensitive employee and company data.
  • Can you integrate payroll data with my accounting software?
    Yes, we ensure seamless integration with popular accounting platforms like QuickBooks, Xero, and others, providing accurate journal entries for your general ledger.
  • What support do you offer if I have questions?
    You will have a dedicated payroll specialist as your point of contact, available via phone or email to answer any questions promptly during business hours.
  • Do you handle payroll for businesses outside of Hamilton Ontario?
    Yes, while based in Hamilton-Vaughan, we provide remote payroll services for businesses across Ontario and Canada, ensuring compliance with all relevant provincial and federal regulations.
