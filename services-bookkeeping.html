How We Work

Our Bookkeeping Process

01
Needs Assessment

We begin by understanding your business operations, transaction volume, and specific bookkeeping requirements — so everything we do is purposefully scoped to your situation.

02
System Setup & Integration

We set up or integrate with your preferred accounting software — QuickBooks, Xero, or otherwise — and establish secure, efficient methods for sharing your financial data.

03
Transaction Recording & Reconciliation

Your dedicated bookkeeper records daily transactions with precision, manages AP/AR, and performs regular bank reconciliations to keep everything in balance.

04
Reporting & Review

You receive accurate financial reports on a regular schedule. We walk through them with you to ensure clarity and make sure the numbers are actually informing your decisions.
